I've been in IT since 1995: retail computer repair, sysadmin, IT director, then more than twenty years running my own MSP. These days I teach what I learned on camera, consult on infrastructure projects, and work with the MSP community at Huntress.

My bias is toward open source, self-hosting, and owning your own infrastructure. If a tool works for you, great. If you work for the tool, that's worth a second look.
